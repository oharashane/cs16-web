Maps

Every map on the server, and what each one asks for: its texture wads, its sky, the models, sprites and sounds its entities name, and its .res file. Red is missing from both the server's content and the Steam files. A missing model shuts the server down when the map loads; a missing sound or sprite is a console error every round; a missing wad is often nothing, because most maps embed their textures. Built by scripts/mapdeps.py --catalogue.
