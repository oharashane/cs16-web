The museum, as of 16 September 2026. The curators' room shows this file; it lives in the repository as `docs/roadmap.md`.

## Now
- **The display.** The rotation and Valve's own maps are up. The curators work through the backlog family by family — classic first, then arena — putting up what plays and is worth a look, with a note and its provenance.
- **Pictures for everything on display.** Made offline from the map files (three views and a plan), so the wings open instantly.
- **Loading times.** The game's first download is 239 MB; the aim is a third of that, by keeping only what the engine ever opens and fetching the rest per map — and one cache shared by the game, the fly-through and the recordings.

## Soon
- **Models on display.** The player skins first: a skin turns on its record and can be worn in your own game.
- **Recordings with their players.** Every recording names who played; the best get a curator's note and a moment to start at.
- **Ratings that mean something.** Enough stars from enough people to sort a wing by them.

## Later
- **The public door.** Visitors without an invitation, once the licence question — bring your own game, verified in the browser — is settled.
- **A second home.** The relay and a server hosted elsewhere, the records staying here.
- **The cheats, as history.** The aimbot and the wallhack of 2003 explained with the files, never installed.
