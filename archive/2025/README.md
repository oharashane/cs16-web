# 2025, as it was

What the project was before September 2026, kept for the record and out of the way:

- `docs-history/` — the 2025 diary, thirty-two files. The review (`docs/review/`) read
  it in full and says what it found.
- `client/` — the 2025 browser client: yohimik's minified 1.0.1 bundle with four sed
  patches, its packaging scripts, and the map lists. Served at `/legacy` until
  10 September 2026; superseded by `web/` (built from source) and `scripts/package-valve.py`.
- `update-valve-zip.sh`, `turbo-userconfig.cfg`, `cs-client-config-README.md` — the
  one-zip packaging and its variants. The client's `userconfig.cfg` moved to `content/`,
  where the packager reads it.
- `AMX_MAPS_MENU_GUIDE.md` — operating notes for the three 2025 servers, retired
  9 September 2026.

Nothing here runs. `docs/engine/journal.md` is the record of what replaced it.
